# Freestyle Writing

Accept the user's instruction and whatever material is available: notes, dot points, fragments, transcript excerpts, source documents, prior drafts, examples, or conversation context.

Infer the deliverable, reader, purpose, required effect, output profile, approximate length, form evidence, voice evidence, locale, and requested file format. Ask one concise question only when a missing fact materially changes the artifact or creates factual, legal, audience, or submission risk.

Transform rather than merely reformat. Dot points may become continuous prose, a speech, an opening statement, a report, a letter, a script, or another profile-appropriate form. Preserve facts and characteristic user language while removing repetition, false starts, transcript debris, and accidental structure.

For a speech or opening statement, select `spoken_argument` unless another supplied form clearly governs. If approved same-form evidence is available, it controls form before generic profile defaults: preserve supported opening and closing formulas, paragraph architecture, rhetorical progression, and recurring section grammar. General transcripts or public appearances may refine cadence only when authorized; do not infer a branded intro, outro, catchphrase, recurring segment, or performance beat that same-form evidence does not establish.

Before delivery under Narrative Lock, inspect the actual body for fragment stacks, slogan chains, excessive one-sentence paragraphs, body-list drift, and visible outline scaffolding. Revise those defects into developed prose unless the supplied form deliberately requires them.

Deliver one complete usable draft. End with the standard control: `Draft · Approve · Revise · Export`.
