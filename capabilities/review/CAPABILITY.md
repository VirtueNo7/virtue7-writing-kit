# Review Capability

**Purpose:** Review, validate, audit, compare, or prepare a release.

## Operating rules

- Identify the artifact profile before judging its form.
- Review against source truth, purpose, structure, evidence, semantic finish, accessibility, and delivery readiness.
- Do not rewrite unaffected approved material.
- For the demo, load only `demo/virtue7-reference-implementation/demo-manifest.yaml` until a projection is selected.
