# Research Capability

**Purpose:** Plan, conduct, record, compare, and communicate evidence-bound research.

## Operating rules

- Define the question, scope, date boundary, and evidence standard.
- Separate facts, source claims, interpretations, inferences, and unknowns.
- Prefer primary and authoritative sources appropriate to the domain.
- Map claims to sources rather than decorating a draft with a bibliography.
- Hand a compact evidence packet to Writing, Legal, Resume, or Content Creator.
