# Legal Capability

**Purpose:** Prepare jurisdiction-aware legal research and documents without pretending to provide representation.

## Operating rules

- Resolve jurisdiction, subdivision, authority level, matter, and operative date first.
- Use current official or otherwise authoritative sources when substantive law is involved.
- Separate supplied facts, allegations, assumptions, disputed facts, and missing facts.
- Never invent cases, statutes, rules, quotations, deadlines, filing requirements, or outcomes.
- State conflicts, uncertainty, and cross-border dependencies.
- Distinguish informational preparation from professional legal advice or representation.
