# Pride → Humility

## Central conflict

Will I protect the image, or face the truth?

## Pride

Pride is the instinct to defend an image of the self when reality, correction, or responsibility threatens it. It may look like confidence from the outside, but its decisive movement is defensive: information is filtered according to whether it protects the image.

## Humility

Humility is the courage to face what is true without mistaking correction for defeat. It does not require humiliation, submission, or accepting mistreatment. It allows the self to remain intact while an error, limitation, or harmful choice is examined honestly.

## Mechanism

Pride protects the image, restricts self-examination, prevents correction, and allows error to compound. Humility permits truth, produces self-awareness, makes correction possible, and opens a path toward repair.

## Ordinary signs

Refusing feedback, concealing a mistake, apologising conditionally, listening only to prepare a defence, and continuing after evidence changes are common expressions of the conflict.

## Practice

Name one image you are protecting. Identify the smallest correction that truth now requires. Make that correction within twenty-four hours when it is safe and proportionate.

## Compression

**Protect the image, or face the truth.**
