# Protect the Image, or Face the Truth

## Cold open

A mistake lasts a moment. The defence of it can last for years.

## Central question

When reality threatens the person we believe we must appear to be, do we protect the image or face the truth?

## Development

Show an ordinary error followed by a sequence of defensive choices: denial, blame, selective memory, and repetition. Each defence offers immediate relief while increasing the eventual cost.

## Contrast

Return to the same first mistake. This time the person pauses, names it accurately, accepts the immediate discomfort, and corrects course before the consequence compounds.

## Decode

Pride is not merely thinking highly of yourself. Here it is the protective mechanism that blocks accurate self-examination. Humility is not weakness. It is the capacity to let truth change the next action.

## Practical close

Ask: What image am I protecting, and what correction becomes possible when I stop defending it?

On-screen line: **Protect the image, or face the truth.**
