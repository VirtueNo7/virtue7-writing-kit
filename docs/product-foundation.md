# Product foundation

Virtue7 is a free, open-source, model-neutral, white-label foundation for writing, research, content transformation, and brief production. Its primary object is an approved meaning system: evidence, claims, decisions, dependencies, constraints, lineage, and unresolved questions that may produce many artifacts without being replaced by any one draft.

## Governing properties

**Collapsible.** A substantial artifact should return to its governing meaning, evidence classifications, decisions, dependencies, qualifications, and open questions. Compression is not permission to remove decisive context.

**Extendable.** Approved state may generate depth, formats, audiences, briefs, and form-native projections. Expansion may add expression and structure, but it may not invent evidence or silently change an approved claim.

**Reversible.** Artifact records preserve source lineage, versions, lifecycle state, and human authority. Reversal is semantic: an earlier governing state can be recovered even when prose cannot be reconstructed word for word.

## Public promise and limits

The public kit contains a usable entry point, compiled route and profile packets, reusable playbooks, state templates, governance schemas, deterministic evaluation fixtures, and release tooling. It works without any private application.

The automated profile checks are structural heuristics. An evidence-sensitive artifact cannot receive release-grade `Pass` without a reviewed evidence attestation, and that attestation still does not prove factual correctness. Human approval is an explicit event bound to an exact artifact version and SHA-256. Independent blinded cross-model semantic evaluation has not yet been completed.

## User levels

- **Quick:** direct work with only material decisions exposed.
- **Guided:** explained choices and visible approval gates.
- **Expert:** sources, assumptions, lineage, gate findings, and unresolved decisions exposed.
- **Builder:** registries, schemas, extension contracts, and compatibility effects exposed.

The same evidence, privacy, approval, and tool-truth rules apply at every level.

## Public/private boundary

The foundation may define generic extension contracts, but it does not include private application code, proprietary orchestration, internal datasets, private briefs, customer state, or application-specific business logic. Extensions must use their own namespace, declare permissions, and may not replace core components by default. Generated artifacts remain white-label and do not require Virtue7 attribution.
