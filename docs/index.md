# AI writing without losing your voice or control

Virtue7 is an open-source, model-neutral runtime for writing, research, creator work, professional documents, and governed production. It accepts direct requests, notes, transcripts, sources, drafts, and saved projects.

[Start in 60 seconds](quickstart.md) · [Browse worked examples](examples.md) · [Understand the architecture](architecture.md) · [Download releases](https://github.com/VirtueNo7/virtue7-writing-kit/releases)

## Why Virtue7

- Task-first: clear work begins without a setup interview.
- Voice-preserving: cadence comes only from authorized evidence.
- Evidence-bound: claims retain their source status and qualifications.
- Form-aware: narrative, research, resumes, manuals, and procedures keep the structure their medium requires.
- Human-controlled: substantive artifacts remain Draft until approved.
- Tool-honest: external actions are never reported as complete without an actual tool record.

Version 0.5.0 includes 35 route-conformance illustrations, 13 governance failure/repair examples, and automated coverage for all 24 output profiles.
