# Release Notes — v0.4.0

## Use AI without losing the way you work

Virtue7 Writing Kit v0.4.0 is a free, open-source, model-neutral content and knowledge-work runtime. Point an AI at the repository or download the ZIP, then ask for the work: fiction, research, episodes, legal and professional documents, product briefs, brand systems, procedures, career materials, reviews, or a workflow of your own. Virtue7 supplies the routing, project state, evidence records, profiles, quality gates, and approval discipline around the host AI.

The release is personal in substance, not decoration. A user can teach the kit approved cadence evidence, choose Form Lock, separate personal and professional scopes, and save recurring workflows as natural-language commands. The same source material can become a product decision, campaign brief, operating procedure, or researched narrative because the role, outcome, structure, evidence standard, and approval chain are explicit.

## Highlights

- personal, role, organization, client, and project profiles;
- optional working identity and social discovery keys with authorization safeguards;
- cadence learning from approved transcripts and prior work;
- Adaptive, Preserve Form, and Narrative Form Lock;
- 32 governed playbooks across eight practical work packs;
- nine synthetic examples, including the same input processed for different users;
- custom commands, routes, templates, profiles, and quality gates;
- evidence, source, claim, contradiction, timeline, and publication-risk records;
- persistent project state and controlled continuation;
- white-label drafts with explicit human approval;
- honest boundaries around live research, transcription, media editing, publishing, analytics, and external actions;
- an updated architecture whitepaper and executable release validation.

Virtue7 does not promise that every AI has tools it does not have. It makes the host more disciplined, more personal, and more reusable—and shows exactly where another tool or a human decision is required.
