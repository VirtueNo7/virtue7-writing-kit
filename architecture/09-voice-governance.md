# Voice Governance Architecture

## Principle

Voice is a set of observable behaviours, not a perfume label.

## Voice contract fields

The contract may include:

- sentence-length distribution;
- paragraph rhythm;
- opening moves;
- argument progression;
- question frequency and purpose;
- narrative distance;
- humour behaviour;
- vocabulary range;
- transition habits;
- repetition tolerance;
- emotional temperature;
- evidence introduction;
- qualification and uncertainty;
- conclusion behaviour;
- prohibited habits;
- supported examples.

## Confidence

Each inferred behaviour receives:

- `high`: repeated across three or more independent creator samples;
- `medium`: repeated but corpus is limited;
- `low`: plausible provisional observation requiring approval.

Low-confidence traits must not become hard rules without user approval.

## Neutralisation rule

Named-author requests must be translated into neutral craft properties. The kit may analyse structure at an abstract level, but it must not reproduce signature expression, distinctive phrasing, or recognisable stylistic combinations.

## Project-native promotion

Approved manuscript units can become voice references. Promotion requires:

1. human approval;
2. successful drift test;
3. provenance record;
4. defined scope of reuse;
5. removal of accidental source mimicry.

This allows the manuscript to increasingly teach the system how the book itself should sound.
