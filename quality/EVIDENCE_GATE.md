# Truth and Evidence Gate

Classify material as supplied fact, verified fact, credible report, quotation, interview statement, interpretation, inference, reconstruction, allegation, disputed fact, theory, or unknown. Do not merge these categories into one confident voice.

For nonfiction, confirm claim lineage, date, source authority, quotation accuracy, and uncertainty. For resumes, confirm every claim against the approved source. For jobs, use posting facts only. For legal work, verify jurisdiction, operative date, authority level, and procedural source.

Structural markers are not semantic proof. An evidence-sensitive artifact may receive release-grade Pass only when a reviewed evidence attestation identifies its scope, reviewer, and source identifiers. The attestation confirms reviewed context, not the truth of every claim.
