# Form Lock Gate

Apply the selected output profile before the lock. In Narrative Lock, block body bullets, numbered-list drift, fragment stacks, pseudo-lists, excessive interior headings, and visible outline scaffolding unless explicitly requested. A pseudo-list is a sequence of short standalone paragraphs or fragments that function as list items without list formatting. Integrate those points into developed prose; when the profile or current instruction permits a list, format them as explicit bullets or numbered items. In Preserve Form, compare against approved form evidence. In Adaptive mode, enforce the selected profile. A legitimate structured artifact may require lists, fields, tables, or steps.
