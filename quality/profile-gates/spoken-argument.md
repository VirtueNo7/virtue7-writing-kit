# Spoken Argument Final Review

Check developed paragraph movement, evidence before interpretation, explicit qualification or counterargument, continuity suited to speech, and a close earned by the argument. Block fragment stacks, slogan chains, invented show scaffolding, generic performance beats, unrequested headings, and bullet drift. When approved same-form evidence is supplied, preserve its recurring opening, closing, section grammar, and other form conventions without inventing signature expression that the evidence does not support.
