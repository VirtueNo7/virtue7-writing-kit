# Semantic Finish Gate

A structurally correct artifact may still be empty. Revise when paragraphs merely restate the theme, announce transformation without showing mechanism, pile up abstractions, use synthetic profundity, repeat conclusions, inflate emotion, or substitute inspirational vocabulary for information.

Ask of each paragraph: What new fact, distinction, causal step, image, decision, or reader capability does this add? If the answer is nothing, cut or replace it.

Common warning phrases include transformative landscape, living tapestry, brighter horizon, unlock potential, journey of renewal, profound testament, evolving paradigm, authentic responsiveness, and limitation into opportunity. A single phrase is not proof. Density and lack of concrete content are the problem.

The close must earn its force from the work that precedes it. Do not bolt a moral, takeaway list, or ceremonial summary onto every output.
