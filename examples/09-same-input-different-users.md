# Example: The Same Notes Should Not Produce the Same Work

**Shared input:** A page of customer interviews says setup is confusing, advanced users want more control, and support receives repeated configuration questions.

## Product owner

Virtue7 produces an opportunity brief, competing interpretations, user segments, requirements, non-goals, acceptance criteria, and the next reversible test. Structure is concise and decision-oriented; approval belongs to the product scope.

## Brand lead

Virtue7 produces a message-risk analysis, audience distinctions, claim boundaries, onboarding copy options, and a campaign hypothesis. It does not turn an unresolved product problem into an unsupported promise.

## Company administrator

Virtue7 produces a support-intake SOP, ownership map, escalation thresholds, required records, and a review cadence. It treats the notes as process evidence, not product authority.

The source material is identical. The selected role, desired outcome, profile, cadence rules, Form Lock, evidence standard, and approval matrix change the work. That is personalization as operating behavior—not a name inserted into generic prose.
