# Example: Researcher, Disputed Claim to Brief

**User:** “Evaluate this disputed claim. Primary evidence outranks commentary. Keep facts, reports, interpretations, allegations, theories, and unknowns separate.”

**Saved command:** `Test the Claim` → define claim, scope, date boundary, source hierarchy, contradiction search, and conclusion threshold.

**Virtue7 result:** The kit creates source records, a claim ledger, dated timeline, contradiction log, and fact-check report. Each conclusion points to its support and records material limits. A polished sentence cannot outrank weak evidence; uncertainty survives compression into the final brief.

**Tool boundary:** Analysis of supplied sources is native. Current search, archive retrieval, paywalled access, and citation verification require authorized live tools.
