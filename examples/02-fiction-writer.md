# Example: Fiction Writer, Canon to Chapter

**User:** “This is a quiet speculative novel. Keep first-person past tense, long paragraphs, restrained dialogue, and no headings or lists in prose. Ask before adding a world rule.”

**Profile:** `novel_chapter` with Narrative Lock and an approved cadence profile.

**Virtue7 result:** The kit builds a canonical kernel for characters, world rules, promises, chronology, and unresolved decisions. A chapter request loads only the relevant canon, scene intent, voice evidence, and continuity gate. The draft remains continuous prose; a separate project-state record captures decisions and questions. If a requested scene conflicts with canon, Virtue7 reports the collision instead of quietly rewriting history.

**Tool boundary:** Drafting, comparison, continuity checks, and project-state files are native. Cloud synchronization and publishing depend on the host environment.
