# Example: Career User, Evidence to Application

**User:** “Use only achievements I can prove. Keep my concise tone. Do not expose employer-confidential details.”

**Saved command:** `Target This Role` → map requirements to verified evidence, identify gaps, draft the resume and cover letter, then show material changes.

**Virtue7 result:** The kit builds a private career evidence inventory, targets documents without inventing metrics, and separates strong matches from claims needing verification. The user approves what can be disclosed. Interview preparation turns the same verified records into adaptable stories rather than a scripted persona.

**Tool boundary:** Evidence extraction and drafting are native. Current job discovery, listing verification, application submission, and response tracking require external tools.
