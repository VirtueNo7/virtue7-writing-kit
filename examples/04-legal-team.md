# Example: Legal Team, Records to Reviewable Matter Packet

**User:** “This client scope is confidential. Use only the supplied records. Separate document facts, party assertions, and analysis. Nothing is final without responsible-lawyer approval.”

**Profile:** Client scope plus matter-specific data classification, jurisdiction rules, authority policy, and approval matrix.

**Virtue7 result:** The kit produces a chronology, exhibit index, issue map, authority table, fact gaps, and draft memorandum. It blocks cross-client reuse, identifies missing jurisdiction or authority, and labels informational output appropriately. Updates are derived from the approved matter state rather than reconstructed from memory.

**Tool boundary:** Structuring and drafting from supplied records are native. OCR, discovery retrieval, citator checks, filing, and legal judgment require external systems and qualified professionals.
