# Example: Brand Lead, Evidence to Campaign

**User:** “Our voice is direct, calm, and technically precise. Avoid inflated transformation language. Every performance claim needs approved substantiation.”

**Saved command:** `Build the Campaign` → load positioning, proof, audience, channel, and approval rules; create one campaign kernel and channel projections.

**Virtue7 result:** The kit turns customer evidence and product truth into a positioning core, claim register, voice contract, campaign idea, and channel-specific assets. The same idea changes shape for a landing page, email, social post, and sales brief while the promise and proof remain stable. Unsupported claims are flagged before release.

**Tool boundary:** Strategy and copy development are native. Competitor monitoring, design, ad-platform publishing, and measurement require external tools.
