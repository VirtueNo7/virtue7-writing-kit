# Example: Company Administrator, Practice to Controlled Procedure

**User:** “This process crosses finance and operations. The SOP must show owners, exceptions, evidence of completion, and escalation—not just steps.”

**Profile:** `standard_operating_procedure`, Preserve Form, organization scope, restricted internal data.

**Virtue7 result:** The kit interviews for the real process, identifies conflicting accounts, and drafts purpose, scope, prerequisites, roles, procedure, decision points, exceptions, controls, records, escalation, and review cadence. Meeting notes update the approved decision state; they do not silently rewrite policy.

**Tool boundary:** Interview planning and document production are native. Recording, transcription, workflow automation, access control, signatures, and task creation require external tools.
