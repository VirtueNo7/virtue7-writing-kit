# Virtue7 Writing Kit v0.5.0

Version 0.5.0 is the final free public Writing Kit release. It takes the lean runtime and complete-example work developed after v0.4.0, keeps the later prose-structure hardening, and deliberately limits the public package to writing-focused capabilities and bounded automation.

The boot is now task-first and mode-first. A boot-only request presents three choices: **Chat**, **Create content**, and **Automate a task**. Clear substantive requests skip the menu and route directly. The runtime then loads only the current capability route, output profile, compact state, and tool contract needed for the step.

The release ships 9 capability modules, 35 routes, and 24 output profiles. It includes the newer `spoken_argument` profile and an explicit pseudo-list guard for prose-led work. Successive short standalone paragraphs that merely enumerate ideas must be developed into connected prose or formatted as actual bullets or numbered items when a list is appropriate.

Governance is stronger than v0.4.0. Approval binds to the exact artifact ID, version, and SHA-256; tool-dependent completion requires a target-specific receipt; artifact lifecycle transitions are validated; and deterministic checks cover privacy, tool truth, Form Lock, career evidence, legal context, claim status, cadence authorization, approval integrity, release state, and pseudo-list drift.

Long-running autonomous runtimes and marketplace-discovery systems are outside the scope of the free public Writing Kit.
