# Craft References

Reference material is dormant by default. Load a small mechanism packet only when the task benefits from cadence, structure, clarity, oral readability, or medium calibration.

Extract transferable craft behaviour rather than identity. Do not store or request imitation of a recognisable creator voice, fictional property, signature rhetoric, proprietary wording, or distinctive presentation system. Prefer neutral observations, short necessary excerpts, source links, project-owned transcripts, and technical descriptions of what the writing must accomplish.
